# Five daily games added on November 19

This field note documents what Best Daily Games played and what we checked on desktop and touch. All five games are by their original creators.

- [Minellion game](https://bestdaily.games/games/minellion/) — test the difference between an unrecognized item and a valid item outside the prompt.
- [Medical Krillion](https://bestdaily.games/games/medical-krillion/) — play the embedded fictional cases, with score and progress shown separately.
- [Chartered game](https://bestdaily.games/games/chartered/) — try the original timed atlas, where time and place each earn points.
- [Contexto game](https://bestdaily.games/games/contexto/) — compare ranks, hints and the English archive.
- [Drink the Ocean game](https://bestdaily.games/games/drink-the-ocean/) — play the original browser version and follow the first water-to-salt trip.

Each guide records its own playtest date, source material and access mode.
